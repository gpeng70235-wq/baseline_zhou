# Geometry angle consistency

The analytic rectangle/hexagon/Case structure is unchanged. Corrected mode rotates the k+2 voltage geometry with the next execution-period center angle `θ(k)+1.5ωTs`. After the analytic sequence is generated, its prediction voltage is independently recomputed from every segment midpoint U3. Legacy mode preserves θ(k). No clipping, candidates, Case rules, or dwell equations were added.
